# The 24 Digital Assets Audit Guide

Based on the Daniel Priestley framework for business leverage. These assets make a business run without the owner.

## IP Assets (Intellectual Property)
1. **Content**: Your library of insights, articles, and videos.
2. **Methodology**: Your unique "way" of solving problems (e.g., a proprietary 5-step process).
3. **Data**: Proprietary information you've gathered about your market or results.
4. **Registered IP**: Trademarks, patents, copyrights.

## Brand Assets
5. **Identity**: Visuals, logos, and recognizable design.
6. **Positioning**: Where you sit in the market relative to competitors.
7. **Values**: What the brand stands for (and against).
8. **Reputation**: Testimonials, awards, and trust in the market.

## Market Assets
9. **Channels**: Owned platforms like email lists or YouTube channels.
10. **Ambassadors**: Influencers or clients who promote you for free.
11. **Lists/Data**: A clean database of leads and customers.
12. **Strategic Partnerships**: Alliances that drive traffic and authority.

## Product Assets
13. **Scalable Core**: Your main product that scales with minimal effort.
14. **High-Value Product**: Premium offerings that drive profit.
15. **Entry-Point Product**: Low-friction products that bring in new customers.
16. **Product Ecosystem**: How different products feed into each other.

## System Assets
17. **IT Systems**: Your tech stack and software setup.
18. **Operations Manual**: Documented SOPs for how the business runs.
19. **Automation**: Workflows that perform tasks without human intervention.
20. **Management Systems**: How you track KPIs and performance.

## Culture Assets
21. **Hiring System**: How you find and vet talent.
22. **Training System**: How you onboard and level up your team.
23. **Core Values (Internal)**: How the team interacts and makes decisions.
24. **Rewards & Incentives**: How performance is encouraged.
